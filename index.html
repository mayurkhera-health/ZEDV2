How this worked for one business

After-school cooking classes, run out of an inbox

Food Explorers runs cooking and baking classes in schools across the Bay Area. Instructor onboarding, each school’s background-check rules, the recipes, the scheduling and every invoice ran through one founder’s inbox.

The part that couldn’t slip was compliance. Every school sets its own requirements before an instructor is allowed on site, and an instructor turned away at the door on the first day of class is a different kind of problem from a late invoice.

  • Onboarding that asks once, instead of over four rounds of email
  • Each school’s compliance rules written down and tracked per instructor
  • One view of the season: who’s locked in, what still needs a decision

Questions owners ask

How much does this cost?

The walkthrough is free. The assessment is a fixed [price to be confirmed]. Most first projects fall between [$X] and [$Y], and you’ll know the exact price before we start. The optional care plan is [$X] a month.

What happens on the free walkthrough?

You tell us about a normal week: where the time goes, what falls through the cracks. We’ll say honestly whether we can help. There’s no slide deck and no pressure to buy anything.

Do I have to replace my software?

Usually not. We start with what you already use, and only suggest something new when it clearly saves more than it costs.

We still use a lot of paper and spreadsheets. Is that a problem?

No. That’s where a lot of small businesses start, and it’s a perfectly good place to begin.

How long does it take?

The assessment takes about [1–2 weeks]. A first project usually takes [2–6 weeks], depending on how many tools are involved.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Accounts are in your name, everything is documented, and we train your team to run it.

What if my tools change, or you’re not around?

Everything is documented so another person could maintain it. If you’d rather not deal with updates yourself, the care plan covers them.

Is my data safe?

We use the access you grant, only for the job at hand, and you can revoke it at any time. [Add specifics once confirmed: where credentials are stored, and who on the team has access.]

Do you work with healthcare or childcare businesses?

Yes, for things like staff onboarding, certifications, scheduling and billing follow-up. We don’t currently handle patient records.

Do you use AI?

Sometimes, where it’s the simplest reliable way to do a job — like sorting incoming documents or drafting a reminder. It always sits inside a process we design, and you approve anything that matters. We pick whatever does the job most simply.

Will this replace my staff?

The goal is to take repetitive work off people’s plates so they can spend time on customers and the work that needs a person.

We’re really small. Is this worth it?

The free walkthrough is how we both find out. If the numbers don’t make sense for your business, we’ll tell you.
