How this worked for one business

After-school cooking classes, run out of an inbox

Kitchen Club runs cooking and baking classes in schools across the Bay Area. Instructor onboarding, each school’s background-check rules, the recipes, the scheduling and every invoice ran through one founder’s inbox.

The part that couldn’t slip was compliance. Every school sets its own requirements before an instructor is allowed on site, and an instructor turned away at the door on the first day of class is a different kind of problem from a late invoice.

  • Onboarding that asks once, instead of over four rounds of email
  • Each school’s compliance rules written down and tracked per instructor
  • One view of the season: who’s locked in, what still needs a decision

Questions owners ask

How much does this cost?

The walkthrough is free. Everything after it is quoted once we have seen how your business actually runs — the assessment after the walkthrough, the first project after the assessment. You get a written quote before any work starts.

What happens on the free walkthrough?

You tell us about a normal week: where the time goes, what falls through the cracks. We’ll say honestly whether we can help. There’s no slide deck and no pressure to buy anything.

Do I have to replace my software?

Usually not. We start with what you already use, and only suggest something new when it clearly saves more than it costs.

How long does it take?

We give you the timeline in writing along with the price, before anything starts. It depends almost entirely on how many tools are involved and how much of the work lives in somebody’s head.

What if my tools change, or you’re not around?

Everything is documented so another person could maintain it. If you’d rather not deal with updates yourself, the care plan covers them.

Is my data safe?

We use the access you grant, only for the job at hand, and you can revoke it at any time.

Do you use AI?

Sometimes, where it’s the simplest reliable way to do a job — like sorting incoming documents or drafting a reminder. It always sits inside a process we design, and you approve anything that matters. We pick whatever does the job most simply.

Show us what takes up your time.

Tell us about the repetitive work, spreadsheets, follow-ups and headaches in your business. We’ll help you find where things could be simpler.
