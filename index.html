    Questions owners ask

    Do I have to replace my software?

    Usually not. We start with what you already use. Replacing something that works costs you money and costs your staff time learning it again — we'd rather connect it to the rest of your business.

    We still use spreadsheets and paper. Is that a problem?

    No. That's a perfectly good place to start. Plenty of well-run businesses operate that way. It just means the first conversation is about what the paper is actually doing for you.

    How much does this cost?

    The first conversation is free. After we understand your business, we'll explain what we'd recommend and give you a clear price before any work starts. No work begins on an open-ended clock.

    How long does this take?

    It depends on the process. We deliberately start with one manageable problem rather than trying to change everything at once — that way you see whether this works for you before committing to more.

    Who owns what you build?

    You do. Accounts should be in your name, the work is documented, and your team is trained on it. If you and we parted ways tomorrow, what we built keeps running.

    What happens if our software changes?

    Everything is documented so it can be maintained — by you, by whoever you hire, or by us. If you'd rather we handle future changes, we can, but you're not stuck with us to keep it working.

    Will this replace my employees?

    That's not the goal. The goal is to remove repetitive work so people can spend more time on customers and on work that actually needs a person. If your staff are spending their week re-typing information, that's the part we'd take away — not them.

    Are we too small?

    That's exactly what the free walkthrough helps determine. If the numbers don't make sense for a business your size, we'll tell you that rather than sell you something.

    Show us what takes up your time.

    Tell us about the repetitive work, the spreadsheets, the follow-ups and the operational headaches in your business. We'll help work out where things could be simpler.
