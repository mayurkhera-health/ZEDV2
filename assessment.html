About the assessment

What if you find nothing worth fixing?

Then we say so, and you don’t pay for the assessment. [Confirm before launch: no charge, or refund.] It doesn’t happen often, but a business that already runs cleanly deserves to hear that rather than be sold a project.

Do I have to do the walkthrough first?

Yes. It’s free, it takes 30 minutes, and it’s how we work out whether the assessment is even the right next step for you.

Who needs to be involved?

You, and one or two people who do the work day to day. We’d rather take twenty minutes of their time than guess.

Will this disrupt the business?

No. Nothing changes while we’re looking. We take notes and ask questions; we don’t touch your systems until you’ve agreed a piece of work.
