What we collect, and what we do with it.

Short version: we collect what you send us so we can reply to you, and we don’t sell it.

Last updated 11 September 2026. This page covers the AutomateSmall website. Work we do inside your business is covered separately, in writing, before it starts.

What this website collects

If you fill in the walkthrough form, we receive what you typed: the kind of business you run, roughly how many people work there, what eats up your time, and your email address.

If you use the 2-minute check, your answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent to us unless you press “Email me this list” or book a walkthrough. There is no account and no sign-up.

We don’t ask for financial details, customer records or staff records through this website, and you shouldn’t send them here.

What we do with it

We use it to prepare for and arrange a conversation with you, and to reply afterwards. If you tick the box asking for occasional tips, we’ll send those too, and every one has an unsubscribe link. That box is unticked by default and booking a call doesn’t tick it.

We never sell or share your data.

Who else can see it

The services we use to run the website, schedule calls and handle email can technically access what passes through them, as with any business. We keep that list as short as we practically can. Ask us and we’ll tell you which ones.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries while there’s a reasonable chance of a conversation, and delete them when there isn’t. Ask us to delete what you sent and we’ll do it and confirm.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us — there’s no form.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies. We use privacy-friendly analytics to count how many people visit and which sections get used. It doesn’t follow you to other websites.
