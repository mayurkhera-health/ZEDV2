Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

Short version: we collect what you send us so we can reply to you, and we don't sell it to anyone.

Last updated 11 September 2026. This page describes how the AutomateSmall website handles information. Work we do inside your business is covered separately, in writing, before it starts.

What this website collects

If you fill in the walkthrough form, we receive what you typed into it: the kind of business you run, roughly how many people work there, what's taking up your time, and your email address.

We don't ask for financial details, customer records or staff records through this website, and you shouldn't send them here.

What we do with it

We use it to prepare for and arrange a conversation with you, and to reply to you afterwards. That's the whole purpose.

We don't sell it, rent it, or pass it to anyone for their own marketing.

Who else can see it

The services we use to run the website and handle email can technically access what passes through them, as is the case for any business. We keep that list as short as we practically can. If you'd like to know which services those are, ask us and we'll tell you.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as there's a reasonable chance of a conversation, and we delete them when there isn't. If you ask us to delete what you sent, we'll do it and confirm when it's done.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we'll handle it — there's no form to fill in.

Cookies

This website doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If we change how any of this works, we'll update this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

Privacy questions go to contact email to be added. The business behind AutomateSmall is legal entity name and registered address to be added.
