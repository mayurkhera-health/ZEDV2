What we collect, and what we do with it.

Short version: right now this website collects nothing at all. When that changes, this page changes first.

Last updated 15 September 2026. This page covers the AutomateSmall website. Work we do inside your business is covered separately, in writing, before it starts.

What this website collects

Nothing, today. The walkthrough form and the “not seeing yours?” box both check what you typed and confirm on screen, but nothing leaves your browser and nothing is stored anywhere — not your email address, not the kind of business you run, not how many people work there.

The 2-minute check keeps your answers in your browser’s session storage so the page can show you a result. They stay on your device and your browser clears them when you close the tab.

The server this site runs on keeps no access log, so your visit isn’t recorded on our side either.

We don’t ask for financial details, customer records or staff records through this website, and you shouldn’t send them here.

Those forms will be connected to a real inbox before we start taking enquiries. This page gets updated before that happens, not after, and it will say exactly what we receive.

What we do with it

Nothing to do yet, because nothing arrives. Once the forms are connected: we use what you send to prepare for and arrange a conversation with you, and to reply afterwards. That is all — there is no mailing list and nothing on this site signs you up for one.

We will never sell your data, and we will never hand it to anyone for their own marketing. That is different from the handful of companies whose software we use to run the site and send email: they can technically see what passes through them, which is what the next section is about. Saying “we never share your data” while that is true of any business would be the easy sentence, not the honest one.

Who else can see it

Today, nobody, because there is nothing to see. This site is a set of static files. There is no database, no account and no sign-up behind it.

Once enquiries are live, the services we use to run the website and handle email will technically be able to access what passes through them, as with any business. We keep that list as short as we practically can. Ask us and we’ll tell you which ones.

How long we keep it

Nothing is being kept. Once enquiries are live, we keep them while there’s a reasonable chance of a conversation, and delete them when there isn’t. Ask us to delete what you sent and we’ll do it and confirm.

Your choices

There is nothing held about you to ask for. Once that changes, you can ask what we hold, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us — there’s no form.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies at all — no advertising, no tracking, none. There is no analytics on it either: nothing counts your visit, and nothing here follows you to other websites. If we add analytics later it will be a privacy-friendly one that doesn’t track you across sites, and this page will say which.

One thing we do want to flag, because “no cookies” is often said and left there. The typefaces on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser fetches them from Google on each page and Google sees your IP address when it does. We don’t get that information and we don’t ask Google for it, but it is a third party seeing a request, so you should know. Serving the fonts from this site’s own server removes it entirely, and if we do that, this paragraph goes.
