Step two

The operations assessment

A short, fixed-price piece of work that answers one question: where is your time actually going, and what is worth fixing first?

What it is

A look at how your business really runs

We spend time with you and, where it helps, with the people doing the work. We follow two or three processes from start to finish — the ones you tell us are eating the most time — and we write down what actually happens at each step.

Then we bring it back to you as something you can read in one sitting.

The practical details

So there are no surprises

  • Fixed price, agreed in writing before we start.
  • Scoped up front — we agree which processes we're looking at.
  • Yours to keep. The map and the recommendations are yours whether or not you hire us for anything else.
  • No obligation to proceed to the next step.

What you actually receive

Six things, in plain English, that you could hand to someone else and they'd understand it.

A map of how the work actually flows

Every step in the processes we looked at, who does it, what tool it happens in, and where it waits.

Where your time is going

An honest estimate of the hours the business spends on each repetitive job, based on what we observed and what you told us.

A list of what could be simplified

Steps that could be removed outright, before anything gets automated.

A shortlist of what to fix first

Ranked by how much time it gives back against how hard it is to do. Usually three to five items.

A recommendation for the first improvement

What we'd do, what it would involve, what it would cost, and how long it would take.

What we'd leave alone, and why

Just as useful. Some things are already working, and some aren't worth the money to change.

What we need from you

Less than you'd think

  • A couple of hours of your time, split across a few conversations.
  • A short conversation with one or two people who do the work day to day.
  • A look at the tools you use — screen-share is fine, we don't need accounts of our own.
  • Honest answers. The workaround someone invented three years ago is usually the most useful thing you can show us.

What happens after

Three options, all of them fine

  • You take the map and do it yourself. Some owners do. It's written so you can.
  • We do the first improvement. Fixed scope, fixed price, agreed before we start.
  • You do nothing for now. The map doesn't expire.

What it costs

It depends on how many processes we're looking at and how many people are involved, so we quote it after the free walkthrough — never before. You'll have a fixed number in writing, and you decide from there. We'd rather give you a real price for your business than a range that turns out to be wrong.
