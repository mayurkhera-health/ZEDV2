How it works

We understand first. Then we simplify. Then we automate what's left.

Most of the disappointing technology projects we hear about started the same way: someone bought a tool before anyone had looked at how the work actually gets done. We do that part first, on purpose.

01

Discover

We sit with you and follow the work. Not the version in the handbook — the version that actually happens on a Tuesday when someone calls in sick.

  • A walk through a normal week, in your words
  • Who touches what, and in what order
  • Where things get re-typed, re-checked or re-sent
  • Which tools you already pay for, and which ones people actually open
02

Simplify

Before we build anything, we take steps out. A step you delete is a step nobody has to run, fix or remember.

  • Approvals that nobody reads
  • Forms that ask for information you already hold
  • Reports that get produced and never opened
  • Two people checking the same thing
03

Automate

Then we take the repetitive parts off people's plates — the sending, the tracking, the reminding, the copying between places.

  • Things that happen on a schedule
  • Things that happen when something else happens
  • Reminders that go out before a date, not after
  • The exceptions get raised to a person — usually you
04

Connect

Where two tools hold the same information, we get them talking so it only has to be entered once.

  • Your existing accounts stay yours
  • We use the connections the tools already provide wherever we can
  • If a tool genuinely can't do the job, we say so before you spend money on it
05

Hand over

The part most people skip. You get everything needed to run, change or move on from what we built.

  • Written documentation in plain English
  • A map of how the pieces fit together
  • A training session for you and whoever else needs it
  • Administration access, in your name
  • A handover conversation where you ask anything you like

What we don't do

It's worth being clear about this early, because it's usually the thing owners are worried about.

We don't sell you a system

There's no AutomateSmall product for you to subscribe to and no seat licences to count.

We don't rip out what works

If your scheduling tool is fine, it stays. We connect to it.

We don't run open-ended projects

Fixed scope, fixed price, agreed before we start. You know what you're spending.

We don't hold the keys

Accounts are in your name. Our access can be removed without breaking anything.
