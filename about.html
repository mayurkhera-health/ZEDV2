Who you'll work with

25+ years solving business problems with technology. Now focused on making that experience useful for small businesses.

I've spent more than 25 years working on how businesses actually run. Processes. Systems that don't talk to each other. Getting information out of one place and into another without a person having to re-type it. Finding the spots where work quietly piles up on one overloaded person and stays there.

Most of that work was through ZEDventures, with larger organisations — the kind that have an operations team, an IT department, and a budget line for improving how things run.

What became obvious over time is that small businesses have exactly the same problems. The paperwork still has to be chased. The invoices still have to be followed up. Someone still has to remember whose certification expires next month, and whether the new hire ever sent back the second form.

The difference is that there's nobody to hand it to. It lands on the owner, usually after closing, usually at the kitchen table.

That's why AutomateSmall exists. The same thinking, without the enterprise price tag, the vocabulary, or the year-long project.

What AutomateSmall is

AutomateSmall helps small-business owners reduce the amount of repetitive operational work they personally have to manage.

We start by understanding how the business actually operates. Then we simplify what can be simplified, take the repetitive parts off people's plates, and get the tools you already use to share information instead of sitting in separate corners.

Then we document it, train you on it, and hand it over.

We are not trying to sell you another large system to run. We are trying to make the business simpler to run.

The connection to ZEDventures

AutomateSmall is built by the team behind ZEDventures. We've taken what we've learned over more than 25 years and made the approach simpler, more practical and appropriate for small businesses.

That's the whole of it. We're not going to put a wall of client logos in front of you, because those engagements aren't yours and they don't tell you anything useful about whether we can help a fourteen-person plumbing company.
