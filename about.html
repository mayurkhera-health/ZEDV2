Over 25 years the same thing kept turning up: a fourteen-person company has the same problems as a four-hundred-person one. The invoices still have to be chased. Someone still has to remember whose certification expires next month.

The difference is who ends up doing it. In a big company that work has a department attached to it. In a small one it lands on the owner, usually after closing.

What AutomateSmall is

AutomateSmall looks at how a small business actually runs, removes steps that don’t need to exist, and makes the rest happen automatically using tools the owner already has.

You get time back, and you see what needs your attention in one place.

There’s no system here for you to buy and then maintain. The point is to make the business simpler to run.

Why a separate brand from ZEDventures

ZEDventures has spent more than 25 years helping organizations improve processes and connect systems — the kind of organizations that have an operations team, an IT department and a budget line for this work.

That experience is the reason AutomateSmall can do this work at all. But the way you sell and deliver to a 400-person company is wrong for a fourteen-person plumbing business, where the person across the table is doing three other jobs while you talk to them.

So it needed its own name and its own prices. That’s the whole story. You won’t find a wall of enterprise client logos here either. Those engagements aren’t yours, and they tell you nothing about whether we can help you.

Who we’re for

Businesses with roughly 3 to 50 people and no dedicated operations, IT or process team — because the owner is usually doing all three jobs.
